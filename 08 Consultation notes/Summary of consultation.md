| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **`Target persona`** | `“Young Filipinos aged 18–25” is too broad to guide the experience.` | `Build a concrete persona with employment, income, expenses, contribution capacity, investment familiarity, and a five-year-plus goal.` | `Use an early-career Filipino with regular income, a five-year-plus goal, and suitable risk tolerance as the primary persona.
