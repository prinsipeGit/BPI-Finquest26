INVESTMENT OBJECTIVE, THEME & THE NEED WE ADDRESS

Objective. The Fund seeks long-term capital appreciation by investing in companies building the infrastructure the next generation of Filipinos is about to use — the power, connectivity, trade, healthcare and enterprise capacity underwriting the first salary, the first home, the first business and the first ₱100,000.

Theme: global thematic — infrastructure buildout. Cross-sectoral and cross-border, spanning energy transition, digital infrastructure, trade, healthcare capacity and digital financial access, expressed through five pillars — EARN · MOVE · LIVE · CONNECT · THRIVE — at 30% Philippines / 65% offshore.

Structure serves the objective. A unitized open-end investment company: fractional units make the ₱100 minimum possible, daily dealing supports peso-cost averaging, and the open-end form lets the manager size positions by conviction and volatility rather than by share indivisibility — which is what makes a risk-budgeted ten-holding portfolio workable at retail scale.

The need. Only 23% of Filipino adults hold any investment, down from 36% in 2021 (BSP). The barrier for ages 18–25 is not price — ₱50 buys a fund on GCash today — but that a standard Client Suitability Assessment scores a young person with no emergency fund as Conservative, barring them from the growth assets a 40-year horizon requires. A young Filipino's human capital is already a 100% concentrated bet on the Philippine economy; 65% offshore is the correct diversification response to what they already own.

CLIENT SUITABILITY

Suitable for investors who:

  • Are classified at least Aggressive under a horizon-weighted risk profile, or have completed the Firsts education-gated unlock;
  • Have an investment horizon of at least five (5) years;
  • Accept that in the worst 12 months of our simulation the Fund fell 22.18%, and will remain invested through it.

INVESTMENT PROCESS — HOW EVERY HOLDING AND EVERY WEIGHT IS DERIVED

STEP 1 THE BUILDOUT TEST — binary gate; all four must pass:

GateRequirement
B1 Buildout≥20% of revenue or 3-yr capex tied to a published national or regional capacity target
B2 LiquidityADTV ≥ ₱30M (PH) / $5M (offshore); free float ≥20%
B3 SolvencyNet debt/EBITDA ≤4.0× or net cash; where net debt is positive, EBIT interest cover ≥2.5×
B4 ConductNo thermal coal >10% of revenue; no UNGC breach

The gate binds. Rejected despite quality: Jollibee, BDO and SM Prime (B1 — demand-led expansion is not a published capacity programme); Semirara and DMCI (B4 — thermal coal); AyalaLand Logistics (redundant — consolidated within Ayala Land).

STEP 2 THE FIRST SCORE — 0–100 conviction:

FactorWt.Measure
F — Future Relevance25%Scale & duration of buildout; backlog
I — Impact on Youth15%Link to a named “first”
R — Resilient Finances25%ROIC, FCF conversion, leverage
S — Sensible Valuation20%EV/EBITDA, PEG vs own history

STEP 3 RISK-BUDGETED WEIGHTING. Weight ∝ FIRST Score ÷ Volatility, water-filled against caps of 12% per security, 15% per conglomerate, 25% per sector and 30% ±3pp Philippine exposure. No weight is chosen by hand. STEP 4 DISCRETIONARY OVERLAY. The manager may deviate ±3pp on valuation or event risk and hold up to 10% tactical cash, with written rationale. Quarterly rebalance, ±2.5pp drift band; expected turnover 30–50%.
